The first module was about driving the tools. This one is about what a senior engineer does with them, and what they refuse to hand over. AI makes it cheap to produce a design document, a code review comment, a list of incident hypotheses or a working solution to an interview problem. It does not make any of them correct, and the cost of a confident wrong answer grows with seniority.

You will see how to use AI to widen the option space in design and review while keeping the decision and the accountability; how to use it to read logs and generate hypotheses during an incident while keeping hard guardrails on production actions; how to learn with AI so that your understanding deepens instead of eroding; what AI-native interviews measure and how this app's assisted mock interview grades direction, verification and critique; and which skills you should keep practising by hand because every other skill depends on them.

Technical skill gets you to the table; it does not get you the offer. Senior candidates who could do the job are turned down, or levelled below where they should be, because their resume reads like a list of duties, their behavioural stories describe the team's work rather than their own, they misread what a round was measuring, or they accept the first number they hear. Each of these is a separate, learnable skill, and none of them is taught by the problem-grinding that most interview preparation consists of.

This module covers the whole path, and it shows the machinery rather than describing it. It starts with the loop: the requisition, interview plan, feedback forms and packet behind it, and one illustrative packet resolved step by step under a debrief and under a hiring committee, so you can see which lines in the forms decide a level. The behavioural lesson runs two stories twice each, weak and strong, with the interviewer's notes beside every line, an anchored rubric scored line by line, the follow-up drill-down, and the write-ups each version produces. A dedicated lesson covers Netflix, Ascend's reference bar, using only what Netflix has published about its culture (checked against the current memo) and the loop shape candidates commonly report, with one story tested against the memo's ideas and rewritten.

The last three lessons cover either side of the loop: how an application moves through an applicant tracking system and a resume role rewritten and re-scored with the bullet formula; how bands, RSU conversion, cliffs, refresher layers, tax at vest and startup preferences shape an offer, with a verified year-by-year comparison and an exercise that stacks grants; and a 13-week plan for the first 90 days with the artefacts that make your work visible. Every candidate, offer and timeline is illustrative, and the lessons say where practice varies by company and country. Practise the interview parts in Ascend's mock interviews at `/interviews`, which run timed coding, system design and behavioural rounds and grade them against the senior bar.

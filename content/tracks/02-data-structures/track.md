Data structures are the vocabulary of every interview and every design review. This track goes beyond "a hash map gives O(1) lookups": you will see how each structure is laid out in memory, why its operations cost what they cost, how real standard libraries implement it, and what breaks when you use it at the wrong scale.

Every lesson has a live visualisation you can step through, an exercise you implement in the browser, and a quiz that checks the trade-offs, not the vocabulary. By the end you will be able to derive the right structure for a problem instead of pattern-matching from memory.

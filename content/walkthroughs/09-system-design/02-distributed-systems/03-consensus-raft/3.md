@0
A lease store, two nodes, A and B, and some clients. The lease is 5 seconds long, renewed every 2 seconds, with a clock drift bound of 500 milliseconds. A leader lease is a time-bounded promise: while a node holds a valid lease, it knows nobody else is leader, so it can serve reads from its own memory without asking anyone. Chubby, Spanner, Raft leader leases and Kubernetes controllers all use one.

@1
A asks the lease store for the lease. Only one node can hold it at a time.

@2
The store grants it until t plus 5 seconds. A records the expiry on its own clock, measured from when it sent the request, which is the conservative end.

@3-4
A client reads, and A answers from memory, with no round trip. Without the lease, A would have to confirm with a majority that it is still leader on every read, which is Raft's ReadIndex, and that costs a round trip.

@5
At t plus 2 seconds, long before expiry, A renews, and the lease now runs to t plus 7. Renewal is a heartbeat with teeth.

@6
Now the failure. At t plus 4, A is cut off from the store and its renewal never arrives. Clients on A's side keep sending reads.

@7
At t plus 7 by its own clock, A stops serving, even though nothing told it to. This is the discipline that makes leases safe: the holder enforces the expiry on itself.

@8
The store sees the lease lapse, but waits an extra 500 milliseconds, the drift bound, before granting a new one, in case A's clock runs slow and A is still serving for a moment.

@9
Then B acquires the lease, until t plus 12 and a half seconds. Between A's step-down and now, nobody served reads. That unavailability window is roughly one lease length plus the grace period.

@10
Clients are redirected to B, which serves reads locally under its own lease.

@11
The second failure mode. If A's clock ran slower than the drift bound, or A paused in the middle of a request, A could still answer a read after B became leader. That is a stale read, the read-side form of split brain. Spanner buys certainty with TrueTime; others add fencing tokens or accept the risk.

@12
So the trade is this. A read becomes one local lookup instead of a quorum round trip. You pay with an unavailability window after a leader crash, where a shorter lease means a shorter outage but more renewal traffic, and with a safety argument that rests on clocks behaving.

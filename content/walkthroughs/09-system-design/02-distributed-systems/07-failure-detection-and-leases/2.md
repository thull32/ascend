@0
A lease store, two nodes, A and B, and clients. A lease is authority granted for a bounded time. Here it is 5 seconds long, renewed every 2 seconds, with a clock drift bound of 500 milliseconds. While a node holds a valid lease it knows nobody else leads, so it can act alone. The question this picture answers: when the holder goes quiet, how does the authority move without two holders at once?

@1-2
A asks the store for the lease, and gets it until t plus 5 seconds. A starts its own clock when it sent the request, not when the reply arrived, because the store started its clock at or after receipt. That keeps A's view of the expiry on the early, safe side.

@3-4
A client reads, and A answers from its own memory, with no round trip, because the lease tells it nobody else can be serving.

@5
At t plus 2, A renews early, and the lease now runs to t plus 7. Renewing well before expiry means a single lost renewal does not cost the lease.

@6
Now A is cut off from the store. Its renewal at t plus 4 never arrives, and clients on A's side keep sending reads.

@7
At t plus 7 by its own clock, A stops serving, even though nobody told it to. That is the discipline that makes a lease safe: the holder enforces the expiry on itself, with no message needed.

@8
The store sees the lease lapse, but waits an extra 500 milliseconds before granting it again, in case A's clock runs slow and A is still serving for a moment. The holder always stops first, and the gap between the two is the price of safety.

@9
B acquires the lease, until t plus 12 and a half. Between A's step-down and now, nobody served. That unavailability window is roughly one lease length plus the grace, which is why the lease length sets a floor on failover time.

@10
Clients are redirected to B, which serves under its own lease.

@11
The case no drift bound covers. If A's clock ran slower than the bound, or A paused in the middle of a request, A could still answer after B became leader: a stale read, the read-side form of split brain. Spanner buys certainty with TrueTime; others add fencing tokens, or accept the risk.

@12
So a lease turns a quorum round trip into one local lookup. You pay with an outage window after a crash, where a shorter lease means a shorter outage but more renewal traffic, and with a safety argument that rests on clocks and pauses behaving.

@0
Three replicas of a counter, each holding a vector with one slot per replica, all zeros. The value is the sum of the slots. Two rules: a replica only ever increments its own slot, and to merge two states you take the larger number in each slot. That merge does not care about order, grouping, or repetition, so every delivery order converges.

@1
N1 counts one like. Its own slot becomes 1. No message, no lock: it is instant.

@2
N2 counts two likes, and its slot becomes 2. The replicas now disagree, N1 says 1 and N2 says 2, and that is allowed.

@3
N3 counts one. And now suppose N1 is cut off from the other two.

@4
N2 sends its state to N3, which merges by taking the maximum per slot: 0, 2, 1, which sums to 3. Nobody's increment was lost or counted twice.

@5
N1 is still cut off, and keeps counting: its slot goes to 2. A replica never has to refuse a write, even during a partition. That availability is the whole point.

@6-7
The partition heals, and N1 sends its vector to both others. Each merges it: their own slots are untouched, because the max with a smaller number changes nothing, and N1's slot jumps to 2. N2 now reads 4, and N3 reads 5.

@8-9
The others send their states back, and N1 merges them too. N1 and N3 read 5. N2 reads 4 for now, because it has not yet heard N3's increment; one more exchange brings it to 5. They would reach 5 in any delivery order, with duplicates, or with messages dropped and resent later.

@10
Why a vector, and not one integer merged with max? Because if two replicas each counted 2, the max of 2 and 2 is 2, and an increment vanishes. Owning a slot is what makes concurrent increments add instead of collide.

@11
There are limits. Decrements need a second counter subtracted from the first. Every replica that ever existed keeps a slot, so churn bloats the state. And an invariant like never exceed 100 needs agreement, which means consensus, not a CRDT.

@12
A text CRDT applies the same three properties to characters. Every character gets an immutable ID, an insert says after this ID instead of at position 1, and deletes leave tombstones, so every replica converges whatever order the edits arrive in, with or without a server.

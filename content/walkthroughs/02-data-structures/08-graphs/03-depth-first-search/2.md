@0
Six vertices, six undirected edges. A connects to B and C, B to D and F, C to D, and D to E. Depth-first search from A commits to one edge at a time and goes as deep as it can. The thing to watch is the stack: it only ever holds the current path from A, never the whole frontier.

@1-3
Enter A at time 1. Its first neighbour is B, so enter B at time 2. B's first neighbour is A again, but that is the edge we just came along, seen from the other end, so DFS skips it. Next is D: enter D at time 3. The path is A, B, D.

@4
D's first neighbour is B, its parent, skipped for the same reason. Then C, which is new: enter C at time 4. The path is now four deep: A, B, D, C. Notice that C is reached through D, not straight from A, because A's edge to C waits in A's list until the whole dive under B is finished.

@5
C looks at its neighbour A. A is already on the path, and it is not C's parent, which is D. That makes C to A a back edge, the one edge that closes a cycle: A, B, D, C, and back to A.

@6
C's other neighbour is D, its parent. Nothing new, so C finishes at time 5 and comes off the stack.

@7-9
Back in D, the remaining neighbour is E: enter E at time 6. E's only neighbour is D, its parent, so E finishes at time 7. Then D has nothing left and finishes at time 8.

@10-11
Back in B, the last neighbour is F. Enter F at time 9; its only neighbour is B, so F finishes at time 10.

@12-13
B finishes at time 11. In A, the edge to C is examined at last, but C is already finished: that edge was classified as a back edge from C's side, so there is nothing more to say. A finishes at time 12.

@14
In an undirected graph every edge is either a tree edge, the ones DFS followed, or a back edge to an ancestor. Here five tree edges and one back edge. The edge to your own parent is the tree edge seen from the other end, which is why the undirected cycle check remembers the parent and skips exactly that one edge. And the stack never held more than four vertices, the length of the deepest path, which is why DFS needs memory only for the current path, and why a long enough chain overflows the recursion.

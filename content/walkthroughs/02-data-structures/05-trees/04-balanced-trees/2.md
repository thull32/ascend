Sorted input, 1 through 7: the input that turned a plain search tree into a chain. This time the tree is AVL, so every node's balance factor, left height minus right height, must stay between minus one and plus one. The labels show each node's height, counted in nodes, and its balance.

@33-37

@39
Six has been lifted over 5 and 7, and the root, 4, is perfectly balanced at zero.

@40
The result is the perfect tree: 4 at the root, 2 and 6 below, and 1, 3, 5 and 7 as leaves. Height three nodes, where the plain tree built a chain of seven. It took four rotations for seven sorted keys, and that rate holds up: for a hundred thousand sorted keys, about one rotation per insert, never more. That is what balancing buys: the input order no longer decides the height, so search, insert and delete stay logarithmic whatever order clients send the keys in.

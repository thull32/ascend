@0
Here is a binary search tree built from nine values, with 8 at the root, 3 and 10 below it, and 4, 7 and 13 down at the bottom. We want its height, counted in edges. One rule does all the work: a node's height is one plus the larger of its two children's heights, and a missing child counts as minus one, so a leaf comes out at zero. A node cannot answer until both children have answered, so the work happens on the way back up.

@1-3
The call starts at the root, 8, and before it can compute anything it asks its left child, 3. Node 3 in turn asks its left child, 1. Watch the call stack grow by one frame with every step down.

@4
Node 1 has no children. Both sides report minus one, so its height is one plus minus one, which is zero. That is the base case anchoring the recursion, and the answer goes back up to 3.

@5-9
Now 3 asks its right child, 6, which has two leaves, 4 and 7. Each leaf does exactly what 1 did: two missing children, height zero, return.

@10
With both answers in hand, 6 computes one plus the larger of zero and zero: height 1.

@11
Node 3 now has both children's answers: zero from 1 and one from 6. One plus the larger is 2. The whole left subtree is finished, and every node in it carries its height.

@12-14
The root turns to its right child, 10. Node 10 has no left child, so it goes straight to the right, 14, and 14 goes left to 13.

@15-16
Node 13 is a leaf, height zero. Node 14 has 13 on one side and nothing on the other, so it takes the larger of zero and minus one: height 1. The minus one convention is what makes a node with a single child come out right.

@17
Node 10 sees minus one on its left and one on its right, so its height is 2.

@18
Back at the root, both sides report 2, so the tree's height is one plus two: 3. That matches the longest path, 8 to 3 to 6 to 4.

@19
Height 3. Every node was visited exactly once, so the cost is linear in the number of nodes, and the call stack only ever holds one frame per level of the tree, four at its deepest here. That is the template for nearly every tree algorithm: a base case for the empty tree, trust the two recursive calls, then combine their answers at the node.

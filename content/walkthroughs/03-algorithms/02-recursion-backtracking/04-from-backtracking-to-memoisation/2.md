@0
Climbing 8 stairs, taking 1 or 2 steps at a time. The last move onto step i came either from step i minus 1 or from step i minus 2, so the number of ways to reach step i is the sum of the ways to reach those two. It is the same recurrence as the call tree, but this time every answer gets a cell in a table, and each cell is filled exactly once.

@1-2
Two base cases. There is one way to stand at the bottom, step 0: do nothing. And one way to reach step 1: a single 1-step.

@3-5
Step 2 is step 1 plus step 0: 1 plus 1, which is 2. Step 3 is 2 plus 1, which is 3. Step 4 is 3 plus 2, which is 5. Each new cell reads two cells that were already filled. Nothing is ever recomputed, because the answer is simply there in the table.

@6-9
The same move all the way up: step 5 is 8, step 6 is 13, step 7 is 21, and step 8 is 21 plus 13, which is 34.

@10
34 ways to climb 8 stairs, after one addition per step. That is linear time, against the exponential tree, and since each cell only reads the two before it, you can keep just those two and use constant space. The numbers are the Fibonacci sequence shifted by one.

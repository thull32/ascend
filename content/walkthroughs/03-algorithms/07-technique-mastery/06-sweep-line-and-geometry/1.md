@0
This is the status structure of the skyline sweep: a max-heap of building heights, stored in an array. The children of position i sit at 2i plus 1 and 2i plus 2, and every parent is at least as large as its children, so the tallest building still standing is always at the root. The sweep pushes a height at each left edge and pops lazily, only when the building at the top has expired.

@1
At x equals 2, the first building starts, height 10. It goes into the first free slot, the root.

@2-3
At x equals 3, a building of height 15 starts. It is appended at position 1, the next free slot, then sifted up: 15 is larger than its parent 10, so they swap. 15 is now the root, and the skyline rises to 15.

@4-5
At x equals 5, height 12 is appended at position 2. Its parent is 15, and 12 is not larger, so it stays. The top is still 15, so the skyline does not change.

@6-7
At x equals 7, the 15 building ends. Pop the root: remove 15, move the last leaf, 12, into the root to keep the tree complete, then sift it down. 12 is already at least as large as its child, 10, so it stops. The skyline drops to 12.

@8-10
At x equals 12, the 12 building ends and is popped, and 10 moves up to the root. But the 10 building ended back at x equals 9. At that time it was buried under 12, so nothing happened; now it surfaces already expired, and is popped too. The heap is empty, and the skyline drops to 0. That is lazy deletion, the same idea as the stale-entry check in Dijkstra.

@11-13
At x equals 15, a new building of height 10 starts, and at x equals 19 one of height 8 is appended below it. 8 is not larger than its parent, so it stays, and the skyline holds at 10.

@14-16
At x equals 20 the 10 building ends: pop it, and 8 moves to the root. The skyline steps down to 8. At x equals 24 the last building ends, and the heap is empty again.

@17
The heights came out in the order 15, 12, 10, 10, 8, and the heap never held more than three. Each push and each pop touches one root-to-leaf path, so it costs order of log n. Every building is pushed once and popped at most once, so after the sort the whole skyline is order of n log n.

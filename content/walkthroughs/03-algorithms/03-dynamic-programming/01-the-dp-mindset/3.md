@0
The memoised version of climbing stairs: ways of i is ways of i minus 1 plus ways of i minus 2, with ways of 0 and ways of 1 both equal to 1, and a memo in front of every call. Main calls ways of 6 with the memo empty. Watch two numbers: how many frames are on the stack, and how many calls ever push one.

@1-5
Ways of 6 is not in the memo, so it pushes a frame and calls ways of 5 first, with its own i of 6 parked in its frame. Ways of 5 does the same for 4, then 4 for 3, 3 for 2, and 2 for 1. Nothing has been computed yet, and the memo is still empty.

@6
Ways of 1 is a base case. It returns 1 without another call. This is the deepest point: seven frames, main plus six ways frames, from 6 all the way down to 1. The memo cannot help here, because none of these states has been solved yet.

@7-9
Ways of 1 returns 1 and goes into the memo. Ways of 2 resumes with its first half, 1, and now needs ways of 0. That is a miss too, so a frame is pushed; it is a base case and returns 1. Ways of 2 is 1 plus 1, which is 2.

@10-11
Ways of 2 returns 2, stored in the memo, and ways of 3 resumes. Its second half is ways of 1, which is already in the memo. A hit returns 1 without pushing a frame, and ways of 3 is 2 plus 1, which is 3.

@12-17
The same rhythm all the way up. Ways of 4 gets 3 from below and finds ways of 2 in the memo: 3 plus 2 is 5. Ways of 5 gets 5 and finds ways of 3: 5 plus 3 is 8. Ways of 6 gets 8 and finds ways of 4: 8 plus 5 is 13. Every second call is a hit, and none of them goes deeper.

@18
Ways of 6 returns 13 to main, and its frame is popped. The memo now holds every state from 0 to 6.

@19
Seven states computed, four memo hits, eleven calls in all, against 25 for the naive tree. The time fell to linear. The depth did not: the peak was seven frames, because ways of n still recurses through n, n minus 1, all the way down before the first base case returns. At n equal to 10 thousand that chain is past CPython's default limit of a thousand frames, and the call raises a recursion error. Memoisation removes repeated work, not depth; tabulation removes both.

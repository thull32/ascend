@0
Every limiter in Ascend runs GCRA, the generic cell rate algorithm, which admits exactly the same requests as a token bucket, so the bucket is the picture to hold in your head. This one has the numbers of Ascend's per-account password bucket: it holds at most 10 tokens and regains one every 6 seconds. A request takes a token, and with none left it is rejected. Capacity sets the burst; the refill rate sets the sustained rate.

@1-10
At time zero, a burst of password attempts. Attempts 1 through 10 each take a token and go through, and the bucket drains from 10 to 0. Ten tries in an instant is the most this account will ever allow at once.

@11-12
Attempts 11 and 12 arrive in the same instant and find it empty. Each is refused with a 429 and a hint saying when to retry. The client pays for the overload, not the API.

@13
Twelve seconds later, 2 tokens have dripped back in, one every 6 seconds. Attempt 13 takes one and goes through, leaving 1.

@14-16
At 18 seconds one more has arrived, so there are 2. Attempts 14 and 15 take them, and attempt 16, in the same second, is refused. Someone guessing hard is held to the refill rate: one attempt every 6 seconds, ten a minute.

@17
Then a quiet stretch. Attempt 17 comes at 54 seconds, 36 seconds later, which has earned 6 tokens. It takes one and leaves 5. Had the pause lasted a minute or more, the bucket would have stopped at its capacity of 10: waiting longer never earns a bigger burst.

@18
Sizing is the trap. Too large a capacity lets a client dump a full bucket, 10 requests, on the API in one instant. Too small rejects legitimate bursts, like a page that fires several calls at once.

@19
A token bucket needs only two numbers per key, the tokens and the last refill time; GCRA gets it down to one, the theoretical arrival time of the next request, which is why Ascend can keep the shared limits in a single Postgres table that every replica checks. The password bucket is keyed per account for unknown devices and per known device otherwise, so a stranger guessing at one learner's email cannot spend the allowance of the learner's own browser.

@0
Bubble sort over the worst possible input: forty values in descending order, from 40 down to 1. Every adjacent pair is out of order, so sorting it takes 39 plus 38 plus and so on down to 1 swaps: 780 in all. Uncapped, that would be 782 frames. The engine stops at 600 frames plus one final limit frame, and this run shows the cap doing exactly that.

@1-39
The first pass. 40 is bigger than its neighbour, so they swap, and 40 moves one step right. It keeps swapping, 39 times, until 40 has bubbled all the way to the end. One swap, one frame.

@40-590
The passes continue. Each one carries the largest unsorted value to the end of the unsorted part, one swap per frame: 39, then 38, and so on. By frame 590, twenty passes are done and the values from 21 to 40 are in place at the right.

@591-599
Pass 21 begins. 20 swaps past 19, then 18, and on down to 11. That is swap 599, in frame 599, and the builder now holds 600 frames.

@600
Swap 600, of 20 past 10, is the one that hits the cap. Its note is thrown away; instead the builder records the state after that swap as the limit frame, with the message "Stopped: frame limit reached", and the generator, checking whether the builder is full, returns. Swaps 601 to 780 are never computed, and the sorted result is never drawn. The cap bounds memory: 601 frames, about 266 kilobytes, in roughly half a millisecond. It does not bound work, though. A generator whose loop never ends would still freeze the page, which is why every generator checks for a full builder itself and stops.

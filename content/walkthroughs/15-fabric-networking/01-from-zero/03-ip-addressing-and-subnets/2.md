@0
This is the third octet of 10.254.19.9, slash 20, the one octet the network boundary falls inside. A slash 20 is two full octets plus 4 bits, so the first two mask octets are 255 and pass the address through, the fourth is 0 and clears it, and only this octet needs binary. The one idea to hold: AND keeps a bit only where both inputs are one, and each column is worked on its own. On screen are the two operands at 8 bits. The address octet, 19, is sixteen plus two plus one: zero zero zero one, zero zero one one. The mask octet, 240, is four ones followed by four zeros.

@1
The operation is AND: the result bit is one only when both bits are one. Work from the rightmost column, the least significant, towards the left.

@2-5
The four right-hand columns are where the mask is zero, the host bits of this octet. Bits 0 and 1 are one in 19 and zero in the mask, so they give zero. Bits 2 and 3 are zero in both, and give zero. Whatever the address held there, the mask clears it.

@6
Bit 4 is the first column where the mask is one, and 19 has a one there too: its sixteen. One AND one is one. This is the only bit of 19 that survives.

@7-9
Bits 5, 6 and 7 are ones in the mask but zeros in 19, so each gives zero. The mask lets those columns through, and there was nothing in them.

@10
The result is zero zero zero one, zero zero zero zero, which is 16. AND with a mask extracts a field: it keeps exactly the bits the mask selects. So the network is 10.254.16.0, slash 20.

@11
That is what the block-size shortcut stands for. 256 minus 240 is 16, so slash 20 networks start every 16 in the third octet, and rounding 19 down to a multiple of 16 gives the same 16 as the AND. The next network starts at 10.254.32.0, so the broadcast is one less, 10.254.31.255, and the hosts run from 10.254.16.1 to 10.254.31.254, 4,094 of them. At Meridian, though, this slash 20 is not a subnet on any wire: it is Singapore's block of fabric links, carved into slash 31s, so those 4,094 hosts are meaningless. And the two wrong answers to avoid: keeping 19 would be a slash 24, and clearing the whole octet would be a slash 16.

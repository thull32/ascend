@0
If your interview language has no heap in its standard library, you write one, and this is everything it must do. A min-heap lives in an array. The children of index i sit at 2i plus 1 and 2i plus 2, and its parent at i minus 1, halved and rounded down. The one invariant: every parent is no larger than its children, so the minimum is always at index 0. We will push 5, 3, 8, 1, 9 and 2, then pop six times.

@1-3
Push 5: it goes into index 0. Push 3: it is appended in the next free slot, index 1, so the tree stays complete, then it sifts up. 3 is smaller than its parent 5, so they swap.

@4-5
Push 8 at index 2. It is not smaller than its parent, 3, so it stays.

@6-8
Push 1 at index 3. It is smaller than its parent 5, so they swap, and now at index 1 it is smaller than 3, so they swap again. 1 is the new root, after climbing the whole path.

@9-10
Push 9 at index 4. Its parent is 3, and 9 is not smaller, so it stays.

@11-13
Push 2 at index 5. It beats its parent 8 and swaps up to index 2, then meets the root, 1, and stops. The array is 1, 3, 2, 5, 9, 8.

@14-16
Now pop. Take the root, 1, and move the last leaf, 8, into index 0 so the tree stays complete. Then sift it down toward the smaller child: of 3 and 2, the smaller is 2, so they swap, and 8 lands on a leaf.

@17-20
Pop 2. The last leaf, 9, moves to the root. Its smaller child is 3, so they swap; then its only child is 5, smaller again, so they swap; and 9 is a leaf.

@21-23
Pop 3. The last leaf, 9, moves up, swaps with the smaller child 5, and stops.

@24-25
Pop 5. Now 8 moves to the root, and it is already no larger than its one child, 9. Nothing moves.

@26-28
Pop 8, and 9 is alone at the root. Pop 9, and the heap is empty.

@29
The values came out in order: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9, with a peak size of 6. Each push and each pop walks one root-to-leaf path, so each costs a logarithm of n. Two things to remember when you write it under pressure: pop moves the last element to the root before sifting down, and sift-down must pick the smaller child. And if n is small, saying you will sort instead, and why, is a legitimate senior move.

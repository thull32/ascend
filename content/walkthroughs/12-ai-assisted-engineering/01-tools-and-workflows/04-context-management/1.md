Eight model calls, about 199,900 input tokens, although no single call sent more than 39,100: the transcript is resent on every call, so the session's cost is the sum of its sizes, and that grows roughly with the square of the number of iterations. This is the input-tokens column of the table. Nothing in the loop removes anything; the only ways down are compaction, a fresh session, or handing a subtask to a subagent.

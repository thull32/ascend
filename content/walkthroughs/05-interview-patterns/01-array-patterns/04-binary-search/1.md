@0
Eight sorted values: 1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 12, 15 and 20, and the target is 12. This is the exact form, with a closed range: low starts at position 0, high at position 7, and both ends are still candidates. The invariant: if 12 is in the array, it lies between low and high, inclusive.

@1
The middle of 0 to 7 is position 3, which holds 7. Seven is less than 12, and the array is sorted, so 12 cannot be at position 3 or anywhere to its left. Low moves to position 4, one past the middle. In the closed form the middle is excluded on both sides, because it has just been checked.

@2
The middle of 4 to 7 is position 5, which holds 12. Found, after 2 comparisons; the worst case for eight elements is 4. The exact form returns as soon as it sees the target, which is its strength and its limit: on an array with duplicates, whichever copy the probes happen to land on is the one you get. When the question is the first or last occurrence, or where a value would be inserted, switch to the boundary form.

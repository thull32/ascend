@0
Six nodes, 1 through 6, and we want the middle in one pass, without counting the length first. Both pointers start at the head. Slow moves one node per step, fast moves two, and the loop runs while fast and fast's next are both non-null. When fast runs out of list, slow has covered half the distance.

@1
Step 1: slow is on 2, fast on 3. Fast has followed two next pointers, slow one.

@2
Step 2: slow is on 3, fast on 5. Four pointers against two.

@3
Step 3: slow moves to 4. Fast steps to 6, the tail, and then follows 6's next pointer, which is null. Six pointers against three: still exactly half.

@4
Fast is null, so the loop stops, and slow is on 4. Six nodes have two middles, 3 and 4, and this guard returns the second. That is what Middle of the Linked List asks for. But Palindrome List, Reorder List and merge sort on a list need the first middle, so that the second half starts at slow's next. For those, the guard is fast's next and fast's next's next, which stops one step earlier, on 3. Odd lengths give the same answer either way, so decide which middle you need before writing the guard, and trace an even-length list to check it.
